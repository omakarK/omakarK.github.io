The Art of Slow Living

Crafted with Quiet Hands and Deep Intention

In our quiet alpine workshop, there are no conveyor belts or synthetic molding dies. Each toy is born from sustainably sourced timber that rested for a full season before meeting the woodcarver’s gouge.

1

Ethical Timber Foraging

We hand-select mature, fallen, and responsibly harvested native hardwood logs from sustainably certified forests.

2

Zero-Nail Japanese & Mortise Joinery

Our toys rely exclusively on interlocking wooden dowels and traditional mortise-and-tenon joints—no metal nails to loosen or rust.

3

Seven-Stage Velvet Hand Sanding

Each curve is rubbed down by hand through seven grits up to 600-grit silkiness. Every edge is radiused so small mouths and hands are perfectly safe.

4

Organic Beeswax & Jojoba Impregnation

Buffed with natural beeswax and cold-pressed organic jojoba oil. 100% edible, antimicrobial, and naturally water-resistant.
