The Art of Slow Handcraft in Kolhapur

In our Jarag Nagar workshop in Kolhapur, Maharashtra, Swapnil and Bhavya Kulkarni lead a dedicated team of generational woodcarvers. Every piece of timber rests for six months in shaded seasoning racks before it is hand-shaped on traditional wood lathes.

1

Ethical Indian Hardwood Selection

We source only mature, naturally fallen, and government forest department-certified Indian Teak (Sagwan) and Sheesham.

2

Zero-Nail Traditional Wooden Joinery

Our toys rely exclusively on interlocking wooden tenons and turned dowels—zero metal nails, zero toxic synthetic glues.

3

Seven-Stage Hand Sanding

Rubbed down with seven progressive sandpapers up to 600-grit velvet softness. Every edge is radiused for small hands.

4

Natural Organic Beeswax & Mustard Polish

Buffed with natural beeswax and cold-pressed edible oils. 100% non-toxic, antimicrobial, and completely safe for teething gums.
